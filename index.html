Case studies

Selected work

01
Agentic AI · Voiro.ai and Genie
AI that revenue teams rely on daily
I kept seeing the same data requests across every client's account notes.
+
The actual problem
  • Revenue data spread across SSPs, DSPs, the ad server, the OMS and Salesforce
  • Every new question became a request to product and engineering
  • At-risk advertisers and revenue drops were spotted late
  • Account managers spent hours pulling reports by hand
What I built and why
  • Prototyped before engineering committed, then shipped in stages
  • An AI agent that answers revenue questions in plain language, with self-serve alerts
  • Scoring that ranks at-risk advertisers and pushes a call list into Salesforce
  • Every change tested against real client questions; ships only above 90%
What changed
  • Prototype to production in 3 months, Voiro's fastest launch
  • Each client team self-serves 8 to 10 reports a day
  • 30% better pipeline conversion for sales
  • Account manager prep from about 2 hours to under 20 minutes
02
Product leadership · AI-native team
Changing how my team builds
Ideas waited months for an engineering slot before anyone could react to them.
+
The actual problem
  • A new idea took 3 to 9 months to reach real feedback
  • Engineering, design and sales reacted to documents, not the product
  • Story quality and release notes depended on manual, senior effort
  • Weekly AI builds started to make the product feel inconsistent
What I changed
  • Made AI prototyping a standard step before design and engineering
  • Built Story Rater, a three-agent check of every Jira story before grooming
  • Built AI-drafted release notes from Jira for engineering, sales and clients
  • Paused new features after launch to put a design system in place
What changed
  • Working prototypes in a couple of weeks; market signal in about two months
  • Release notes in under 2 minutes, down from 30 to 40
  • New PMs write sprint-ready stories without senior review
  • Engineering owns how it's built; product owns what and why
03
0 to 1 · Self-serve marketplace
Brand Center, and shutting it down
Direct ad sales still ran over email.
+
What made it hard
  • Two-sided product. The advertiser workflow and the publisher workflow have almost nothing in common
  • Every publisher had a different ops setup. Couldn't copy one and generalise
  • First three discovery calls gave us three different problem definitions
  • No benchmark. Had to define what "good" looked like before building toward it
How I approached it
  • Discovery across six publisher teams before writing a single requirement. Patterns only emerged at interview five and six
  • Prototyped the advertiser flow and publisher dashboard separately, validated each before joining them
  • Deliberately scoped out self-serve campaign editing in v1. The instinct was to build it, the right call was not to
  • Recruited beta partners early: Zee5, JioCinema, Paytm, PhonePe, Rooter
Where it landed
  • Closed beta with Zee5, JioCinema, Paytm, PhonePe and Rooter in under 9 months
  • Adoption came in 90% below target
  • Made the call to shut it down, kept the code for reuse and documented why it fell short
  • The lesson I carry: set kill criteria before every launch, not just success metrics

Decisions I'd make again

Making Voiro enterprise-ready

SituationEnterprise IT teams flagged separate logins and slow access removal as a security risk.

The callTalked to IT admins at two clients, then shipped Google and Microsoft SSO tied to their directories.

ResultEnterprise onboarding cut from 3 days to 1; access ends when someone leaves.

Security features aren't a differentiator. They're table stakes.

Saying no to the AI wishlist

SituationSales brought a wishlist of AI features to win demos.

The callWeighed each on customer value, reuse and run cost: built some, folded some into existing work, declined the rest in writing.

ResultA roadmap that fit our capacity, and a second retail client served by extending an existing system instead of building a new one.

Solve the problem behind the request, not the request.

Going async with engineering

SituationLine-item creation timed out and created duplicates for large publishers.

The callMapped every failure mode with the engineering lead, then made the case for async.

ResultTeams create 100 line items at once; duplicates and tickets dropped.

Bring engineering the problem, not the solution.

Catching drops before clients do

SituationClients found revenue drops before we did.

The callBuilt daily reconciliation against a 7-day average, with proactive client alerts.

Result30% fewer support tickets. Voiro now calls clients first.

Observability is the foundation, not a feature.
